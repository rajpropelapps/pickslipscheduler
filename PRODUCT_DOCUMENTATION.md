# PickSlip Scheduler

## Smarter warehouse work planning, in one view

PickSlip Scheduler gives warehouse managers a clear, practical way to organize picking work. It brings unassigned pick slips, picker schedules, and an AI warehouse analyst together in one easy-to-use workspace.

Instead of relying on spreadsheets, whiteboards, or disconnected operational tools, managers can see what needs to be picked, assign the right person, and keep the day's work moving from a calendar-style schedule.

## The challenge

Warehouse picking is a fast-moving operation. Managers need to balance incoming work, picker capacity, and changing priorities while avoiding duplicate assignments and schedule conflicts. When scheduling lives across paper, spreadsheets, and informal conversations, it becomes difficult to answer basic questions quickly:

- What work is still unassigned?
- Who is already scheduled?
- Which picker can take the next job?
- Are two tasks competing for the same person and time?

PickSlip Scheduler is designed to make those answers visible and actionable.

## A calendar built for picking operations

The Scheduler provides a calendar-style timeline where picker names appear as rows and work is placed against the hours of the day. Managers can use the view to understand the operation at a glance, then assign work in the way that feels most natural to them.

### Assign work your way

PickSlip Scheduler supports multiple assignment workflows:

- Drag an unassigned pick slip onto a picker's timeline.
- Right-click a picker time slot and select an unassigned pick slip.
- Switch to Assign Picker view and drag a picker onto a pick slip.
- Right-click a pick slip and choose a picker without dragging.

These options give users flexibility while preserving one consistent set of scheduling rules.

### Focus on the work that still needs attention

Only unassigned pick slips appear in the available-work lists. Once work is assigned, it is immediately removed from those lists and shown in the picker schedule. This reduces clutter and helps managers focus on the next decision rather than manually tracking what has already been handled.

### Filter to the information that matters

Managers can filter the scheduler by:

- Date
- Picker
- Move order
- Item number
- Item description

The same filters are available across both assignment views, making it easy to move from a broad operational overview to a specific task.

## Built-in scheduling safeguards

PickSlip Scheduler applies operational controls automatically:

- A pick slip or pick-slip line can have only one active assignment.
- A picker cannot be assigned overlapping work during the same time period.
- Default work duration is 30 minutes, supporting a standard warehouse planning cadence.
- Conflicts are clearly reported before the schedule is changed.
- Successful assignments are saved permanently and remain available after a page refresh or application restart.

These safeguards help managers schedule with confidence while preserving the flexibility to adjust work as the day evolves.

## AI Warehouse Manager Analyst

The integrated AI Warehouse Manager Analyst turns operational data into quick answers. It provides a familiar chat experience for asking questions about current work, picker schedules, and unassigned pick slips.

Example questions include:

- Which pick slips are unassigned?
- Which picker has the most scheduled work?
- What work is scheduled for a specific picker?
- Are there any scheduling conflicts?

The assistant is grounded in the current pick-slip, picker, and assignment data. It is designed to provide concise operational guidance rather than guesswork.

## Data visibility for warehouse teams

PickSlip Scheduler works from warehouse move-order and employee data. It organizes pick-slip headers and line items into a scheduling-ready view, including key information such as:

- Move order and line numbers
- Item number and description
- Required and delivered quantities
- On-hand quantity
- Required date
- Picker assignment and scheduled pick time

This creates a shared operational picture without requiring users to manually reconcile multiple data sources during the workday.
